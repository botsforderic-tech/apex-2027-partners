What is APEX

Seven events.
Four categories.
One score.

APEX measures raw athletic ability across seven events and turns it into one number: your APEX Score. Sprint faster, express more power, lift more, go longer, and your score climbs.

The format

One day, combine style, on an outdoor track and field.

The athletes

Pro, Olympic and collegiate athletes beside hybrid athletes and weekend warriors.

The category

Not a fitness race or a workout. An athletic competition.

The APEX app

Always on.
Always training.

Athletes don't just show up on competition day. They train for it in the APEX app, following programs built for each stop and coached by APEX coaches like Joe Pedulla.

  • A program for every stop. APEX Ready, APEX Miami and APEX Miami Elite, running 4 to 8 weeks at up to 6 sessions a week.
  • Scores and results live here. Athletes track their APEX Score and results in the app and on the web.
  • Growing with the season. New programs and features roll out through 2027, keeping athletes in the app between stops.
  • Room for partners. Product moments, presented programs and offers delivered where athletes train every day.
